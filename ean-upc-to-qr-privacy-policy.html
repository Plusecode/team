Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

EAN UPC to QR is provided by Barzig Lab Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you convert EAN, UPC, GTIN, or other supported barcode formats into GS1 Digital Link QR codes.

Summary

EAN UPC to QR does not require an account, does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide optional features that you choose to use, such as Google Sheets integration.

The app primarily processes barcode information provided by you to generate GS1 Digital Link QR codes and related conversion data.

Information the App Processes

Camera Barcode Scanning

When you use the camera scanner, camera frames are processed on your device to detect supported barcode formats, including UPC, EAN, GTIN, and GS1 barcodes.

The app does not upload camera images or video to Barzig Lab Inc.

Image Barcode Scanning

When you choose an image from your device, the selected image is processed on your device to detect supported barcode formats.

The app does not upload the selected image to Barzig Lab Inc.

Barcode Conversion

When a barcode is detected, imported, or entered manually, the app may process the barcode value to:

  • Validate barcode structure and check digits.
  • Convert UPC, EAN, or GTIN values to GTIN-14.
  • Generate GS1 Digital Link URLs.
  • Generate GS1 Digital Link QR codes.
  • Create conversion status records, error messages, reports, or export files.

These conversion operations are performed locally on your device unless you choose to use an optional cloud-connected feature, such as Google Sheets synchronization.

CSV Import and Export

The app may allow you to import barcode lists from CSV files and export conversion results, QR codes, or reports.

Imported and exported files are stored only on your device unless you choose to share, upload, or save them using another application or service.

Google Sheets Integration

Google Sheets integration is optional. The app only accesses Google Sheets if you choose to connect your Google account and grant permission through Google's authorization process.

Google Permissions (Scopes) We Request

When you connect your Google account, the app requests the following Google API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — allows the app to view and manage the Google Sheets spreadsheets you choose to use with the app.

You will see this permission listed on Google's own consent screen before you grant access, and you can decline or stop the connection at any time.

Google Data the App May Access

If you authorize Google Sheets access, the app may read barcode-related rows from spreadsheets selected or authorized by you and may write conversion results back to those spreadsheets.

The app may read or write information such as:

  • Product name or item description.
  • Original EAN, UPC, or GTIN value.
  • Detected barcode type.
  • GTIN-14 value.
  • Generated GS1 Digital Link URL.
  • Conversion status.
  • Error messages.
  • File name or export reference for generated QR codes.
  • Last updated timestamp.

The app does not access your Gmail, Google Drive files outside of Sheets, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any other Google product or data type.

How Google Data Is Used

Google Sheets data is used only to provide the user-facing features shown in the app:

  • Importing barcode lists you select from a connected spreadsheet.
  • Converting the EAN, UPC, or GTIN values found in that spreadsheet.
  • Writing generated GTIN-14 values and GS1 Digital Link URLs back to the spreadsheet.
  • Recording conversion status and error messages in the spreadsheet so you can track progress.

Barzig Lab Inc. does not use Google Sheets data for advertising, marketing, profiling, building user profiles, training AI or machine learning models, or selling personal information. We do not use Google user data for any purpose other than the app features described above.

Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure of Google Data

Barzig Lab Inc. does not share, sell, rent, transfer, or disclose Google user data to any third party, advertiser, analytics provider, or data broker.

Google user data is transmitted directly between your device and Google's own servers using Google's official Sheets API, over an encrypted connection. It does not pass through, and is not routed to, stored on, or otherwise accessible to, any server operated by Barzig Lab Inc. Google itself processes this data as the Google Sheets service you have chosen to use, subject to Google's own privacy practices.

The only circumstances in which we would disclose Google user data are: (a) with your explicit direction or consent, (b) to comply with a valid legal process such as a court order or subpoena, or (c) to investigate or prevent security incidents, fraud, or abuse. Outside of these circumstances, no one other than you and Google has access to this data.

Data Protection for Sensitive Data

The app applies the following safeguards to protect your Google account data:

  • Encryption in transit: All communication with Google's APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Secure credential storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored only on your device, using your device's secure, encrypted storage facilities (such as Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage). Tokens are never transmitted to or stored on Barzig Lab Inc. servers, because Barzig Lab Inc. does not operate any backend server for this feature.
  • Minimum necessary access: The app requests only the single Google Sheets scope needed to provide the feature, and only after you explicitly grant consent through Google's own authorization screen.
  • No server-side storage: Because the app communicates directly with Google's API from your device, Barzig Lab Inc. has no server-side infrastructure that stores, logs, or otherwise has access to your Google account credentials or spreadsheet contents.
  • Limited Use compliance: Our access to and use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Human Access to Google Data

Barzig Lab Inc. does not routinely access or review your Google Sheets data. Human access, if ever required, is limited to situations such as responding to your support request, investigating security or abuse issues, complying with applicable law, or when you have explicitly granted permission.

Storage and Retention of Google Data

Spreadsheet content itself is not stored or retained by Barzig Lab Inc. at any point — it is read from and written to Google Sheets in real time, directly between your device and Google's servers, and is not persisted on our side.

To support app functionality, the app may temporarily cache a small amount of data locally on your device, such as the spreadsheet ID, selected sheet name, and the results of your most recent conversion. This local cache is retained only until it is overwritten by your next sync, you clear it from within the app, or you uninstall the app.

The underlying data in your Google Sheets remains in your Google account, governed by your Google account settings and Google's own privacy practices, until you delete or modify it yourself.

Deleting Google Data

You can delete Google user data associated with the app at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling the app, which removes the locally cached spreadsheet ID, sheet name, and recent conversion results described above.
  • Editing or deleting the relevant rows or spreadsheets directly in Google Sheets or Google Drive.
  • Revoking the app's access to your Google account (see "Revoking Google Access" below), which immediately and permanently stops the app from reading or writing any further data.

Revoking Google Access

You may revoke the app's Google account access at any time from your Google account security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions). After access is revoked, the app will no longer be able to read from or write to your Google Sheets unless you authorize access again.

Scan and Conversion History

The app may store recent scan and conversion history locally on your device so that you can reopen, review, or export previous conversions.

This history may include barcode value, barcode type, GTIN-14, generated GS1 Digital Link URL, product name if provided, conversion status, error messages, and scan or conversion time.

This history is not sent to Barzig Lab Inc.

Permissions

Camera Access

Used only to scan barcodes.

Internet Access

Used for optional features such as Google Sheets synchronization and authorized spreadsheet data exchange.

Vibration Access

Used to provide scan feedback.

Image Picker Access

Used only when you choose an image to decode a barcode.

Data Sharing

Barzig Lab Inc. does not sell personal information.

Barcode conversion operations are performed locally on your device unless you choose an optional cloud-connected feature.

If you choose to use Google Sheets integration, selected spreadsheet data is exchanged directly with Google's own servers, only as authorized by you and only for the purpose of providing the app's spreadsheet import, conversion, and synchronization features. Barzig Lab Inc. does not share, transfer, or disclose this data to any other third party. See the "Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure of Google Data" section above for details.

Data Retention

Local scan history, conversion history, and application data are stored on your device.

You may delete local app data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

Google Sheets data remains in your Google account until removed by you.

Children's Privacy

EAN UPC to QR is not directed to children under 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
