About Barcody

by Barzig Lab Inc.

Barcody is a small, focused toolkit for people who work with barcodes every day — makers, sellers, warehouse teams, and developers. It grew out of a simple frustration: generating a clean, print-ready barcode or converting a UPC into a modern GS1 Digital Link QR code shouldn’t require a design suite or an enterprise contract.

What we build

  • The barcode studio — generate thirteen barcode symbologies, from Code 128 and EAN/UPC to GS1 DataMatrix, PDF417 and Aztec, as crisp vector SVG, PDF, EPS, or PNG.
  • The GS1 Digital Link converter — turn a GTIN/UPC into a standards-based Digital Link QR code, one at a time or hundreds at once from a CSV.
  • Android apps — Barcode QR Generator – Bulk, Nutrition Scan Diet, Diabetes and EAN UPC to GS1 QR, all on Google Play.

How it works

Barcodes are rendered by our own barcode engine — the same one that powers our Android apps — so what you download is exactly what our scanners read back. Anything sensitive stays with you: images you scan and CSV files you convert are processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded to our servers.
