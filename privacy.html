Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Barzig Lab Inc. takes your privacy seriously. This policy covers our apps: VectorBarcode, EAN UPC to QR, and Nutrition Lookup. None of our apps require an account, and we do not sell personal information. Where possible, processing happens entirely on your device.

VectorBarcode

VectorBarcode is designed to work entirely on-device. Barcode generation and scanning are performed locally — no barcode data is uploaded to external servers.

Data we do not collect

  • Barcode values or data you enter
  • Scanned barcode contents
  • Generated images or exported files
  • Your History or Favorites lists
  • Camera images or video

Data stored on your device

VectorBarcode stores your barcode History, Favorites, notes, and app settings locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to Barzig Lab or any third party. You may delete it at any time from within the app.

Analytics

VectorBarcode does not include any third-party analytics SDKs. We do not track app usage, feature usage, or user behavior.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the platform app store (Google Play). Barzig Lab does not receive or store payment card details. Please refer to the platform's privacy policy for details on payment data handling.

EAN UPC to QR

EAN UPC to QR converts EAN, UPC, GTIN, and other supported barcode formats into GS1 Digital Link QR codes. Camera scanning, image scanning, and barcode conversion are performed locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

EAN UPC to QR offers an optional Google Sheets integration that runs only if you connect your Google account and grant permission. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For the full details of camera and image scanning, barcode conversion, Google Sheets access, data retention, and permissions, see the full EAN UPC to QR privacy policy →

Nutrition Lookup

Nutrition Lookup lets you scan or enter a product barcode to look up nutrition information. Camera frames and entered barcode values are processed to identify the product and retrieve its nutrition data. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Because nutrition data is retrieved from a product information source, the app may use an internet connection to send the scanned barcode value for the purpose of returning matching nutrition information. It does not upload camera images or video. Recent lookups may be stored locally on your device so you can review them, and you can clear this history from within the app or by uninstalling it.

For the full details of camera and image scanning, product lookup, permissions, and data retention, see the full Nutrition Lookup privacy policy →
